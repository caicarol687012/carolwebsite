Professor Ma Wenguo

马文国教授简介

马文国，教授，武学博士，古典武艺传习者，原西安体育学院武术系主任，国际汉唐学院武艺研究所主任，长安唐刀馆创始人。自幼习武，精研翻拳、劈挂、戳脚、八极、缠海鞭杆、劈挂刀、绨袍剑、武当剑，独流通臂拳、两翼通臂拳、意拳、猿功、苗刀、疯魔棍、地趟剑、奇枪、陆合枪法、六剑等传授，耳提面命之余又潜心于古典武艺之典籍，勤习不辍，对古典武艺之演练及实战格斗和劈刺颇有心得。2005年开始兼习拳击、泰拳、剑道、击剑和菲律宾魔杖等武技，和中国武术进行比较研究，致力于“古典武艺”之研习和传承。

Ma Wenguo is a professor, a doctor of martial arts, and a practitioner and teacher of classical martial arts. He is former Head of the Wushu Department at Xi’an Physical Education University, Director of the Martial Arts Research Institute at the International Han-Tang Academy, and founder of the Chang’an Tang Sabre Hall. He began training as a child and received instruction in, and studied in depth, Fanquan, Pigua, Chuojiao, Bajiquan, Chanhai biangan, Pigua sabre, Tipao sword, Wudang sword, Duliu Tongbei, Liangyi Tongbei, Yiquan, Ape Exercises (猿功), miao dao, Fengmo staff, Ditang sword, Qi spear, Liuhe spear and the Six Swords, among others. Beyond this personal instruction he has devoted himself to the classical texts of martial arts, practising without pause, and has gained deep insight into the performance of classical martial arts, practical combat, and cutting and thrusting. From 2005 he also trained in boxing, Muay Thai, kendo, fencing and Filipino stick fighting, comparing them with Chinese martial arts, and has dedicated himself to the study and transmission of “classical martial arts”.

曾多次应邀赴美国、土耳其、新加坡、比利时和日本等国家传授翻拳、鞭杆和苗刀等古典武艺。2005年，执教上海市武术队，为优秀运动员传授绨袍剑及劈挂刀、劈挂拳等武艺。2017年11月，执教于山西省武术队，传授劈挂拳、绨袍剑和劈挂刀、奇枪等经典套路，指导运动员进行“传统武术”和“竞技武术”的“融会”，效果明显，受到好评。2009——2011年，担任西安体育学院泰拳队总教练，多次组队参加全国泰拳锦标赛，获得优异成绩。多次受邀赴大学、企业和社区主讲武术文化专题讲座，传授擒拿、劈刺及格斗术，在全国性的专业武术培训班上，讲授古典武艺，传授古典武艺之拳法及兵器训练。2013年起，多次受邀赴西安市特警支队和西部战区、中部战区某特种部队传授兵器之劈刺实战，提高部队战斗力。2017年，福建泉州南少林寺武僧北上长安，学习疯魔棍，传授武僧少林棍法。

He has been invited many times to the United States, Turkey, Singapore, Belgium, Japan and other countries to teach classical martial arts such as Fanquan, biangan and miao dao. In 2005 he coached the Shanghai Wushu Team, teaching elite athletes the Tipao sword, Pigua sabre, Piguaquan and other arts. In November 2017 he coached the Shanxi Provincial Wushu Team, teaching classic routines including Piguaquan, Tipao sword, Pigua sabre and Qi spear, and guiding athletes to “integrate” “traditional wushu” with “competitive wushu”, with clear results that were well received. From 2009 to 2011 he was head coach of the Muay Thai team at Xi’an Physical Education University, leading teams to the national Muay Thai championships several times with excellent results. He has often been invited to universities, companies and communities to lecture on martial arts culture and to teach seizing and locking, cutting and thrusting, and combat techniques; at national professional wushu training courses he has lectured on classical martial arts and taught their fist arts and weapons training. Since 2013 he has been invited many times to the Xi’an Special Police Detachment and to special forces units of the Western and Central Theater Commands to teach practical weapon cutting and thrusting, improving their combat effectiveness. In 2017, warrior monks from the Southern Shaolin Temple in Quanzhou, Fujian, came north to Chang’an to learn the Fengmo staff, and he taught them Shaolin staff methods.

2018年起，拜西北著名天启棍大家卢建军先生为师，系统学习和传承天启棍， 正式开始研习西北棍法。

From 2018 he became a disciple of Mr. Lu Jianjun, a renowned master of the Northwest’s Tianqi staff, systematically learning and carrying on Tianqi staff and formally beginning his study of Northwestern staff methods.

擅长古典武艺之劈挂、翻拳及猿功的演练、格斗，对枪法和刀剑之劈刺亦有独到研究。

He excels in the performance and combat application of the classical arts of Pigua, Fanquan and Ape Exercises, and has original insight into spear methods and the cutting and thrusting of sabre and sword.

常年笔耕不辍，每年有百万文字见诸网络和报端，以武学文章、散文和随笔为主，文笔朴实生动，隽永醇厚。

He has written steadily for many years, publishing a million characters a year online and in newspapers, mainly martial arts articles, essays and informal writings, in a style that is plain and vivid, lasting and rich.

2017年，在西安创立“长安唐刀会”，出任总教习，致力于古典武艺之单双手刀法传承与推广。

In 2017 he founded the “Chang’an Tang Sabre Society” in Xi’an and serves as its chief instructor, dedicated to carrying on and promoting the single- and two-handed sabre methods of classical martial arts.

2014年，进入影视圈，参与多部电影、电视剧的拍摄，饰演主要角色。

In 2014 he entered film and television, taking part in several films and TV series in leading roles.

2020年1月，荣登美国《功夫》杂志封面人物。

In January 2020 he was featured on the cover of the American magazine Kung Fu.
