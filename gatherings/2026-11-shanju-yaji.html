Three-Day Martial Arts Gathering

苍岭清舍 · 三日武学雅集

深秋时，山色已斑斓。苍岭清舍隐于群山之间，听松风穿林，看云影漫过山峦。正是秋光可赏、故友宜聚的时节。

In late autumn the hills turn to many colours. Tucked among the mountains, 苍岭清舍 listens to the pine wind moving through the woods and watches cloud shadows drift across the ridges. It is a season for admiring autumn light and for old friends to gather.

幸得武学博士马文国教授莅临山中。先生博通古今武艺，深谙武学之道，如此难得的相聚，岂宜独享？不如邀来邻里亲朋、同修同好，共赏一山秋色，聆听先生论武，亲身习艺，让这份相遇化作三日山居雅集。

We are fortunate to welcome Professor Ma Wenguo, a doctor of martial arts, to the mountain. Widely versed in martial arts old and new, and deeply grounded in their principles, he offers a rare meeting too precious to keep to ourselves. So we invite neighbours, family, friends and fellow practitioners to share the autumn hills, hear the Professor speak on martial arts, practise with him in person, and turn this encounter into a three-day mountain gathering.

融贯诸家 · 武学无界

Many Traditions, One Art · Martial Arts Without Borders

马文国教授，原西安体育学院武术系主任，国际汉唐学院武艺研究所主任，古典武艺传习者。

Professor Ma Wenguo is former Head of the Wushu Department at Xi’an Physical Education University, Director of the Martial Arts Research Institute at the International Han-Tang Academy, and a practitioner and teacher of classical martial arts.

自幼习武，数十年潜心研习中国传统武学，不拘门户，广涉诸家。精研翻拳、劈挂、八极、通臂等拳法，以及苗刀、剑术、棍法、鞭杆等传统兵器，亦兼习拳击、泰拳、剑道等世界武技，融会比较，探究武艺之共通。

He began training as a child and has devoted decades to traditional Chinese martial arts, ranging freely across schools rather than keeping to one. He has studied fist arts such as Fanquan, Pigua, Bajiquan and Tongbei in depth, along with traditional weapons including the miao dao, sword, staff and biangan, and has also trained in boxing, Muay Thai and kendo, comparing them to explore what all martial arts share.

其学既深于传统，又不止于传统；既重技艺之精，也重武学之理。

His learning is rooted deeply in tradition yet not bound by it; he values both refined technique and the principles behind it.

因人施教 · 众人共修

Teaching to Each Person · Practising Together

本次雅集不设僵化的固定课程，而是尊重每位来宾的身体特点与学习兴趣，在轻松互动中，共同决定三日的研习内容。

This gathering has no rigid, fixed curriculum. Respecting each guest’s body and interests, we will decide together, in a relaxed and interactive way, what to study over the three days.

第一日上午｜识身选桩

Day One, Morning | Know Your Body, Choose Your Stance

从太极无极桩、八极两仪桩、意拳平抱桩等简单桩法切入，在亲身体验与交流中，认识身体、筋骨、气血养生与传统武学的关系。

We begin with simple standing postures such as the Taiji Wuji stance, the Baji Liangyi stance and the Yiquan embracing stance. Through hands-on practice and conversation, we explore how the body, sinews and bones, qi-and-blood wellbeing and traditional martial arts relate to one another.

马教授将结合每个人的身体条件，指导选择适合自己的日常桩功。

Professor Ma will guide each person, according to their own physical condition, in choosing a daily standing practice that suits them.

接下来两天半｜共选一艺

The Next Two and a Half Days | Choose One Art Together

经过半日交流，大家共同投票，从适宜的拳法、棍法等传统武艺中，选出呼声最高的一门，由马教授带领大家集中研习。

After the first half-day, everyone votes together, choosing from suitable traditional arts such as fist forms and staff. The most popular choice will be studied in depth under Professor Ma’s guidance.

一人一桩，因身而异；众人一艺，共学共进。

A stance for each person, fitted to each body; one art for all, learned and grown together.

愿三日之后，每个人既找到一种适合自己的日常练习，也收获一门可以继续研习的传统功夫。

We hope that after three days, everyone will have found a daily practice that suits them, and gained a traditional art they can keep studying.
